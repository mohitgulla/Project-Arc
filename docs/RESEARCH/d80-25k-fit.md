**Fees** (`config/costs.yaml`): $0.0403 per contract per side (ORF + OCC + CAT) plus TAF/SEC on sells. Fees are per contract, so they are the same share of a trade at any account size; slippage (`slippage_frac` 0.25 × quoted spread) is the real cost and is also per contract.

## 5. PDT is moot

- No profile in `config/account_profiles.yaml` sets `day_trades.rule: pattern_day_trader` (profiles: cash_debit, cash_long_only, margin; with it: none). The gate's `account_profile_day_trades` rule only runs for such a profile, so it never fires.
- Alpaca paper `/v2/account` (read-only probe, 2026-10-10, all three key pairs) returns no `pattern_day_trader`, `daytrade_count` or `daytrading_buying_power` field. FINRA retired the PDT rule on 2026-06-04.
- `DayTradeRule` (`arc/account_profiles.py`) now carries a one-line comment saying so; no behaviour change.

## 6. Owner decisions, if any

1. **Keep `max_alloc_pct` 0.075 or raise it.** At $25,000 it lets 13/22 past opens and 91% of menu items through, and caps settled-cash concurrency at ~13 full-size positions. The opens it drops are the high-priced names (1 lot needs $1,990–$4,705, i.e. 8.0%–18.8% of equity). Raising it fits more of them but means fewer concurrent positions and a larger single-name loss as a share of the account.
2. **Never-fit names** (measured: META, AMD, MU, PANW; estimated: SNDK): leave them on the list (they idle and cost a shortlist slot when Research picks them) or drop/exclude them so the slots go to names that can trade. The core list ones (META, AMD, MU) are owner-set via `universe`.
3. **`max_open_positions` 8** no longer binds (cash does). No change needed; noted so nobody expects 8 full-size positions.
4. Nothing else needs a decision: no absolute-$ floor fails at $25K, fees are a rounding error, PDT is gone.
